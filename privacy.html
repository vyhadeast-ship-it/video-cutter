Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

1. Information We Process

Video Cutter is a desktop application that processes video content and helps users prepare it for short-form social media publishing.

Depending on how you use the application, information processed may include video files, video metadata, generated titles and hashtags, and information required to connect supported social media services.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Process and convert video files.
  • Create shorter video clips.
  • Generate title and hashtag suggestions.
  • Connect supported social media accounts.
  • Publish content when explicitly authorized by the user.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the application.
  • Protect the application and its users from misuse.

3. TikTok Integration

Video Cutter may use the official TikTok Content Posting API to allow users to publish videos to TikTok.

Users must authorize the connection through TikTok. Video Cutter does not request or store a user's TikTok password.

Only permissions necessary for the supported publishing functionality are requested.

4. Storage

Video files processed by Video Cutter are primarily handled locally on the user's computer. Authentication information may also be stored locally when required for connected services.

5. Third-Party Services

Video Cutter may interact with third-party services, including YouTube, TikTok, and other social platforms supported by the application.

Those services have their own privacy policies and terms. Users should review the policies of services they choose to connect.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be transmitted to a connected social media platform when the user chooses to publish content through that platform. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when necessary to protect the security of the application and its users.

7. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the application. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

Video files and generated content may remain on the user's device until the user deletes them. Authentication information may remain for as long as necessary to maintain an authorized connection or until revoked or deleted.

9. Children's Privacy

Video Cutter is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that personal information has been provided by a child, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

10. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal information. You may also withdraw authorization for connected services where applicable.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
