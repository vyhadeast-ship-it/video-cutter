# Privacy Policy **Last updated: September 9, 2026** Video Cutter ("Video Cutter", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the Video Cutter desktop application and its integrations with third-party services such as TikTok. ## 1. Information we process Depending on how you use the application, Video Cutter may process the following information: ### Video content Video Cutter may process video files or video URLs that you provide to the application. Video files may be temporarily stored on your computer while they are downloaded, converted, processed, or divided into clips. ### Social media account information When you connect a supported social media account, the application may receive authorization information required to perform actions that you explicitly request. For TikTok, this may include an access token and an account identifier provided through TikTok's authorization system. ### Application information The application may process basic technical information necessary to operate its integrations, including authentication tokens, configuration information, and information required to communicate with third-party APIs. ## 2. How we use information Information processed by Video Cutter is used to: * Process and convert video content. * Create shorter video clips. * Generate titles and hashtags. * Connect the application to supported social media services. * Publish content when explicitly authorized by the user. * Maintain and troubleshoot the application's functionality. * Protect the security of the application and its integrations. We do not use social media account information for purposes unrelated to the functionality requested by the user. ## 3. TikTok information Video Cutter may use TikTok's Content Posting API to allow users to publish videos to their TikTok accounts. The TikTok integration operates only after the user has authorized the application. Video Cutter does not intentionally collect TikTok passwords. Authentication is handled through TikTok's authorization system. Video Cutter uses only the permissions necessary for the functionality provided by the application. ## 4. Storage of information Video files processed by the desktop application are primarily handled locally on the user's computer. Authentication information required for integrations may be stored locally by the application when necessary to maintain an authorized connection. Users can remove locally stored application data and revoke authorization through the relevant third-party platform. ## 5. Third-party services Video Cutter may interact with third-party services, including: * YouTube, when users provide YouTube video URLs. * TikTok, when users connect and publish content through TikTok. * Other social media platforms that may be integrated into future versions of the application. These services have their own privacy policies and terms of service. Video Cutter does not control the privacy practices of third-party services. ## 6. Data sharing Video Cutter does not sell personal information. We do not intentionally share personal information with third parties except when necessary to provide a functionality explicitly requested by the user, operate an integration, comply with legal obligations, or protect the security of the service. For example, when a user explicitly chooses to publish a video to TikTok, the content and information necessary for that publication are transmitted to TikTok through its API. ## 7. Data security We take reasonable technical measures to protect information processed by the application. Authentication credentials and access tokens should be kept confidential and should not be intentionally shared with other users. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. ## 8. Data retention Video files may remain on the user's computer until the user deletes them or the application removes temporary files according to its processing workflow. Authentication information may remain stored locally while an integration remains authorized. Users may revoke third-party authorization or remove locally stored application information when applicable. ## 9. Children's privacy Video Cutter is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to the application's functionality. ## 10. Your rights Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights to: * Request access to personal information. * Request correction of inaccurate information. * Request deletion of personal information. * Object to or restrict certain processing. * Withdraw consent where applicable. To make a privacy-related request, please contact us using the contact information provided with the application. ## 11. Changes to this Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date. ## 12.
