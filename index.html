Video Cutter Turn videos into vertical short-form content Video Cutter is a desktop application designed to help creators transform video content into vertical short-form videos suitable for modern social media platforms. The application can download video content from a provided source, convert it to a vertical 9:16 format, process the video into shorter clips, and prepare the resulting content for publication on supported social media platforms. Features Video processing Video Cutter automatically processes videos into a vertical 9:16 format designed for short-form platforms. Clip creation Long videos can be divided into shorter clips so creators can work with individual pieces of content. Automatic titles and hashtags Video Cutter can generate suggested titles and hashtags for the resulting clips. Social media publishing Video Cutter is designed to allow users to publish their processed videos to supported social media platforms, including TikTok. When publishing through an integrated platform, the user remains in control of the content and the account to which it is published. TikTok integration Video Cutter uses TikTok's Content Posting API to allow authorized users to publish video content directly to their TikTok account. The user must explicitly authorize the application and approve the publishing action. Video Cutter does not publish content to a user's TikTok account without the user's action and authorization. User control Video Cutter is designed so that users maintain control over the content they create and publish. Before publishing content through an integrated platform, users can review the generated content and publishing information.
